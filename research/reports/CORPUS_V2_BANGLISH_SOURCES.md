# Corpus v2: Banglish and Bengali source decisions

## Rule and conclusion

**ACCEPT** means that the artifact and its upstream text rights permit transformation, commercial use, and public redistribution. **EVALUATION-ONLY** means no training use. **REJECT** means neither training nor redistribution from the artifact. A Hugging Face, repository, or compilation license does not clear copied comments, reviews, news, or books. Apache-2.0 may cover banglatok's curation, but CC text retains its own license and attribution. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en) permits commercial adaptation with attribution/change notices; [CC BY-NC-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/legalcode.en) and [CC BY-ND](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nd/4.0/legalcode.en) fail this release gate.

No reviewed natural social/review Banglish corpus is presently rights-cleared for public v2 training redistribution. **BanglishRev is evaluation-only, and synthetic transliteration is prohibited.**

## Acquisition and leakage order

1. Build a deny index from **all** BanglishRev reviews/replies and all official benchmark dev/test surfaces before acquisition.
2. Acquire scoped Pralekha government text, then a small direct Global Voices pilot, Storybooks, Wikimedia supplements, and only novel licensed Tatoeba rows.
3. Prefer quality/traceability over volume. Seek a written commercial redistribution grant for the Bangladesh Romanized Corpus; do not substitute generated Banglish.
4. Preserve owner, revision/hash, row/page ID, URL, author/translator, source license/policy, attribution, origin status, and transformations per row.
5. Exact- and near-dedupe after Unicode/case/spacing/punctuation/URL normalization. A match removes its whole connected group: product/thread, article/translations, wiki page family, story, Tatoeba graph, or Pralekha `n_id`.
